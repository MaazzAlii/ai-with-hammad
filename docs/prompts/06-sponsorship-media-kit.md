# 06 — Sponsorship & media kit

## Sponsorship (`/sponsorship`)
`PageHeader` ("Partner with <site>", intro) with CTAs "Start a partnership" (anchor to form) + "View media kit".
Blocks in order (each hides when empty): Who we are · Audience (combined followers with explanation, age / country /
gender bars — admin-entered percentages with "as of") · Content categories (accent badges) · Platforms · Top content ·
Campaign examples · Previous partnerships (logo, date, summary) · Formats/packages (deliverable ticks, platform badges,
rates notice — **never** show internal rates) · Why partner · Inquiry form in a `glass-panel`.

## Media kit (`/media-kit`)
Same blocks, arranged for print: `PrintButton` in the header, `print-break` before Platforms and Partners,
`print-avoid` on cards. In print, glass and the ambient layer are removed automatically.
